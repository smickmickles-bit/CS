Slide 4

Audience Segments

❄️ COLD AUDIENCES (Prospecting)

  • Residents aged 25–65 within 30km of corridor postcodes
  • Interest: Local community, infrastructure, public transport, urban development
  • Demographic layering: Zone A skews 25–45; Zone B skews 45–65
  • Parents with children (Newcastle for Kids overlap)

🔥 WARM AUDIENCES (Retargeting — Post Pixel)

  • Website visitors — 30-day window
  • Video viewers — 25%, 50%, 75% thresholds
  • Facebook Page engagers — 90-day window
  • Event responders on Facebook

👥 LOOKALIKE AUDIENCES

  • 1%–3% LAL of website visitors (AUS)
  • 1% LAL of lead form completers
  • 2% LAL of video viewers (75%+)
